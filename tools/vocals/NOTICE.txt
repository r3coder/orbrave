==============================================================

The vocal audio files (the teto_* words, made by build_vocals.py into server/vocals/) are excluded from this project's licence
(AGPL-3.0-or-later, which covers the source code) and from the CC0 dedication of the other samples.

  voicebank's terms of use (重音テト音声ライブラリー使用許諾条件):
  https://kasaneteto.jp/guideline/voice.html (non-commercial use only).
- Melodies and lyrics: written for ORBRAVE and dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0
  (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). They are the notes and lyrics in
  tools/vocals/make_ustx.py: anyone may sing, play or reuse them, with any voice, for any purpose.

The CC0 dedication covers the melodies and lyrics only, not these recordings of Teto's voice.
The audio files are not part of this repository or the built site: they are served only by
ORBRAVE's share server (server/vocals.mjs) to its orb page. They may be played only as part of
ORBRAVE (its orb page and its exports of songs made there); no permission is given to copy,
extract, reuse or redistribute them outside this project. A fork gets no vocal audio; rendering its
own with the voicebank (tools/vocals/) is under the voicebank's terms, not this project's (it may
also sing the CC0 melodies with another voice).

They are whole 4-bar lines sung with the voicebank (works made with it), not the voicebank itself:
no recording of the voicebank is included. How they are made: tools/vocals/ (see
public/samples/SOURCES.md).
